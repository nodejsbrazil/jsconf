# Code of Conduct

## 🛡️ Community Commitment

At JSConf Brasil, we believe in creating a safe, welcoming and respectful space for everyone. This applies to speakers, attendees, organizers, volunteers and sponsors.

By participating in JSConf Brasil, you agree to follow this code of conduct. The organizing team commits to enforcing it seriously. Your collaboration is essential.

---

## ⚡ TL;DR

**We do not tolerate harassment of any kind.**

Everyone should feel welcome, regardless of:

- Gender, gender identity or expression
- Sexual orientation
- Disability
- Physical appearance or body size
- Age
- Ethnicity or skin color
- Religion (or lack thereof)
- Technologies or programming languages used

**Be respectful. Don't be a jerk.**

---

## 🚫 Examples of unacceptable behavior

- Offensive or demeaning comments
- Sexual imagery in any context of the event
- Intimidation, stalking or deliberate harassment
- Inappropriate physical contact or unwanted sexual attention
- Aggressive interruptions during talks, panels or discussions
- Embarrassing or non-consensual photography

> This applies to all areas of the event: talks, hallways, parties, booths, social media, etc.

---

## 💼 Sponsors and booths

Sponsors and representatives must follow this code in full. **The following are not permitted**:

- Materials, giveaways or activities with sexual connotations
- Uniforms or costumes that are sexualized
- Environments that promote objectification or exclusion

---

## 🧯 In case of a violation

If someone acts abusively or disrespectfully, the organization may intervene with:

- Formal warning
- Badge removal and expulsion from the event, without refund
- Contacting authorities, if necessary

Come to us immediately if you:

- Experience or witness any form of harassment
- Feel that something is wrong
- Need help or want to talk

> The team will be identifiable by specific t-shirts.

---

## 🤝 We support you

If needed, we will:

- Escort you to a safe location
- Contact security or medical support
- Help with transportation or any other basic support

---

## 🗺️ Where this applies

This code applies to:

- All official JSConf Brasil activities
- Side social events promoted by the community
- Online groups and channels related to the event

---

JSConf Brasil is built by all of us. Take an active part in keeping our environment positive, collaborative and diverse.
